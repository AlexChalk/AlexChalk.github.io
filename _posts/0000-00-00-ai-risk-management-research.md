The papers below all explore potential improvements to AI risk management. Risk management is central to the EU's AI Act, and given that this law entered into force in August 2025, risk management is also immediately applicable to real-world AI governance questions.

I expect that all of the following papers will be comprehensible to public servants with a non-technical background (but if you fit that description and you disagree, please let me know).

*Safety Frameworks and Standards, Ziosi et al., Oct 2025, [[link]](https://aigi.ox.ac.uk/publications/safety-frameworks-and-standards-a-comparative-analysis-to-advance-risk-management-of-frontier-ai/).*



*The science and practice of proportionality in AI risk evaluations, Mougan et al., Feb 2026, [https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10017).*



*Open Problems in Frontier AI Risk Management, Ziosi et al., April 2026, [https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25982](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25982).*



*Prioritization of Risks from Artificial Intelligence, Saeri et al., June 2026, [https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04490](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04490).*



*A Methodology for Quantitative AI Risk Modeling, Murray et al., Dec 2025, [https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08844](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08844).*



*The Role of Risk Modeling in Advanced AI Risk Management, Touzet et al., Dec 2025, [https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08723](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08723).*



*Toward Quantitative Modeling of Cybersecurity Risks Due to AI Misuse, Barrett et al., Dec 2025, [https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08864v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08864v1).*



Please feel free to contact me if you have questions on any of the above.
